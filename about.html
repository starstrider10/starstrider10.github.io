The Artist

Creating for physical spaces & lasting archives.

My work is driven by the pursuit of quiet visual depth—whether scaling granite ridges in the Cascades at dawn, documenting high-altitude weather patterns, or capturing intimate portraiture under fleeting natural light.

I believe physical fine art prints transform how we inhabit our living and working spaces. Each print is crafted to bring mountain air, natural textures, and timeless calm into interior environments.

Craft & Technical Process

Built for clarity, dynamic range & archival stability.

Every photograph is captured using high-resolution full-frame sensors and prime lenses calibrated for edge-to-edge sharpness. Color grading prioritizes filmic skin tones, natural foliage hues, and deep atmospheric shadows.

For physical prints, color profiles are standardized in sRGB and ProPhotoRGB color spaces to guarantee that physical pigment prints match digital gallery previews precisely.
